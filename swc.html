The four Saturdays

What you'll do

Every session is hands-on, and every exercise uses a real task from your own work or life.

  1. 01

    Ask for what you actually want

    A five-part way to tell an AI tool what you need. Write a request the way you normally would, rebuild it, and compare the two answers yourself.

  2. 02

    Say what you mean

    An exercise with no AI at all: a partner follows your instructions exactly as written, and the gaps show up fast. Then you fix your request.

  3. 03

    Catch the mistakes

    A plain-language picture of how these tools come up with answers. Then test AI on facts about your own neighborhood, where you're the expert and can spot what it makes up.

  4. 04

    Put it to work

    Use AI as a helper, a researcher and a critic, and decide for yourself which of its suggestions to keep. Finish one real task from start to finish.
